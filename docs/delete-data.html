Delete Your Data

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Important: Shikai stores your GitHub data locally on your device. The only data that leaves your device is anonymous crash and performance diagnostics sent to Sentry, described below.

How to Delete Your Data

You have two options to completely remove all your data from Shikai:

Option 1: Sign Out

Signing out of Shikai immediately deletes:

  • Your GitHub OAuth token
  • Your Personal Access Token (if configured)
  • All cached repositories, contributions, and activity
  • Your user profile data
  • Your theme preferences
  • All other locally stored data

To sign out: Open the app > Profile tab > Settings (gear icon) > Sign out

Option 2: Uninstall the App

Uninstalling Shikai from your device removes all local data permanently.

Data That Is Not Stored

Shikai does not store any of the following on external servers:

  • Your GitHub credentials
  • Your repositories or code
  • Your contribution history
  • Your personal information

Crash Reports

Released builds send anonymous crash and performance diagnostics to Sentry. These reports carry no name, email, GitHub username, or account id, and your tokens are stripped before they leave the device. Because a report cannot be tied to you, there is nothing to look up and delete for a single user. Reports age out on Sentry's retention schedule. See the privacy policy for the full description.

Data Retention

Your GitHub data is stored locally on your device only. When you sign out or uninstall, all of it is immediately and permanently deleted.
